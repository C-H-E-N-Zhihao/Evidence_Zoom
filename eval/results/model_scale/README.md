# Model scale × input: findings (claude-zhihao, 2026-11-21)

Numbers: [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) (generated by `experiments/model_scale/analyze.py` from the `run_*.json` here).
Charts: [tokens](frontier_visual_tokens.png), [GPU-s](frontier_visual_gpu_s.png) (`eval.plot_frontier`).
Setup: 36 visual questions on 12 synthetic accident forms; Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct (A100) and
Qwen2.5-VL-72B-Instruct-AWQ (H100) served by vLLM on RCP; temperature 0; $0. Accuracy is MEASURED.

## What the numbers say

1. **The right crop beats a bigger model on the whole page.** 7B on the OCR-anchored crop: 61% at
   11.7 TFLOPs/q. 72B on the full page: 56% at 316 TFLOPs/q. The small model on the crop is as accurate or
   better at **~1/27 of the compute** (FLOPs ≈ 2 × params × tokens, an estimate; the GPU types differ, so
   GPU-seconds are not comparable across the two models).
2. **With the right crop, the large model is perfect.** 72B on the anchor crop: **36/36 (100%)** at
   104 TFLOPs/q, against 56% at 316 TFLOPs/q on the page: +44 points at ~1/3 of the compute.
3. **The vision model cannot choose the crop itself, at either size.** Asked to pick grid cells on a
   434×536 overview, the chosen region covers the evidence in 0/24 (7B) and 3/36 (72B) cases. A 72B selector
   with a 7B reader (E2) is no better (3/36). Allowed to answer from the overview, both models answer directly
   and never zoom (overconfident). So a model-free selector is the necessary component; on these forms it is
   the OCR anchor (`pipeline/anchor`, claude-andrew-b), which covers 36/36.
4. **Remaining 7B errors are reading errors**, not selection errors: q5 ("did A tick 8?") 8/12 on the anchor
   crop vs 12/12 on the gold crop. The anchor crop for q5 is a 3-row mosaic; the 7B misreads which row is
   ticked. q6 (sketch) 5/12 for 7B even on the gold crop: a reasoning limit (who hit whom), not a pixel limit.

## Design consequence

Default route: **OCR answer → OCR-anchored crop → (no anchor) overview fallback**. Model size is chosen by
the accuracy needed: the 7B crop route beats the 72B page route; the 72B crop route is the accuracy ceiling.
Model-chosen zoom stays only as the last-resort fallback for unknown layouts, labelled as unreliable.

## Caveats

Synthetic forms with one layout; 36 questions (one wrong answer = 2.8 points). AWQ 4-bit 72B. Compute is a
FLOPs estimate. Generalisation to real scans and other layouts is not measured here.
